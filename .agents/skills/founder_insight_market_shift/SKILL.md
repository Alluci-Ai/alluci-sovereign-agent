# Founder Insight & Market Shift Discovery (`fnd_02`)

## 1. Overview & Strategic Mandate

This skill defines the operational, cognitive, and analytical sequence for the **Alluci Sovereign Agent** to execute **Founder Insight & Market Shift Discovery**.

### Strategic Purpose
Many founders can clearly describe *what* they built. Far fewer can clearly articulate *why* they were the ones to build it, *what unique insight* led them there, and *why the market is finally ready today*.

Great companies emerge because a founder recognized:
- A changing customer behavior or cultural transition.
- A broken industry assumption or false paradigm.
- An overlooked market pattern or structural friction.
- A technological, AI, or regulatory shift.
- A new business model or distribution mechanism.
- A future state that others had not yet fully recognized.

**The Objective:** Transform founder intuition, lived experiences, and earned market truths into a persistent **Strategic Operating Narrative System** that aligns founders, leadership teams, investors, customers, partners, and future Alluci agents.

## 5. Decision Framework & 5-Tier Confidence Matrix

### 7-Tier Decision Hierarchy
When evaluating competing strategic options or positioning directions, Alluci MUST prioritize using the following hierarchy:

$$\text{1. Truth} \succ \text{2. Founder Authenticity} \succ \text{3. Customer Value} \succ \text{4. Evidence} \succ \text{5. Strategic Differentiation} \succ \text{6. Long-Term Value} \succ \text{7. Simplicity}$$

### 5-Level Evidence & Validation Matrix
- **Level 1 — Founder Perspective:** Founder beliefs, personal experiences, vision, intuition, strategic hypotheses. *(Discovery input; requires validation).*
- **Level 2 — Customer Evidence:** Customer interviews, user feedback, discovery sessions, surveys, pilot customers, testimonials. *(Validates customer need).*
- **Level 3 — Market Evidence:** Industry reports, competitive analysis, market research, trend analytics, independent publications. *(Validates market opportunity).*
- **Level 4 — Business Evidence:** Revenue, customer growth, product adoption, retention, partnerships, expansion metrics. *(Validates business viability).*
- **Level 5 — Strategic Evidence:** Product-market fit, repeatable customer outcomes, independent validation, defensible moats, sustainable economics. *(Validates strategic durability).*

### 5-Tier Decision Confidence Matrix
Every major recommendation produced by Alluci MUST explicitly report its Decision Confidence Level:

1. **Very High Confidence:** Supported by multiple independent sources, strong customer validation, business performance data, and historical precedent. Minimal uncertainty.
2. **High Confidence:** Supported by strong evidence, customer validation, market research, and strategic reasoning. Some uncertainty remains.
3. **Moderate Confidence:** Supported by limited customer evidence, emerging market trends, strategic hypotheses, or early product validation. Additional validation recommended.
4. **Low Confidence:** Relies primarily on founder assumptions, limited market data, or unvalidated hypotheses. Treat as an experiment.
5. **Unknown:** Insufficient information exists. Alluci recommends research rather than speculation.

## 6. The 10-Step Operational Execution Protocol

Alluci follows this exact 10-step sequence:

### Step 1 — Initiate Discovery
- Confirm workflow objectives, participating founder(s), and business context.
- Review existing company documentation and define target deliverables.
- **Output:** Discovery Plan & Initial Strategic Hypotheses.

### Step 2 — Capture Founder Intelligence
- Conduct structured discovery sessions to map personal journey, career experiences, values, motivations, defining moments, and long-term vision.
- **Output:** Founder Intelligence Profile & Values Matrix.

### Step 3 — Discover Strategic Insights
- Analyze customer frustrations, industry observations, broken assumptions, contrarian beliefs, and emerging technology shifts.
- Core Question: *What does this founder understand that the broader market has not yet fully recognized?*
- **Output:** Insight Inventory & Pattern Analysis.

### Step 4 — Analyze Market Shifts
- Evaluate customer behavior changes, technology evolution, AI advancements, regulatory developments, and economic trends.
- Separate temporary trends from long-term structural shifts.
- **Output:** Market Shift Analysis & Timing Assessment.

### Step 5 — Validate Founder-Market Fit
- Assess domain expertise, customer credibility, unique access, industry relationships, conviction, and strategic timing.
- **Output:** Founder-Market Fit Assessment.

### Step 6 — Architect the Opportunity
- Define customer opportunity, market opportunity, category creation opportunity, competitive opportunity, and strategic MOAT.
- Formulate the core **Market Thesis** and **Category Thesis**.
- **Output:** Opportunity Architecture.

### Step 7 — Validate Evidence & Score Confidence
- Evaluate all claims against the 5-Level Evidence Matrix and assign confidence levels (Very High to Unknown).
- **Output:** Evidence Matrix & Decision Confidence Score.

### Step 8 — Generate Strategic Intelligence
- Synthesize the **Strategic Intelligence Package** containing 30+ reusable organizational knowledge assets.
- **Output:** Strategic Intelligence Package.

### Step 9 — Human Review & Approval
- Present findings to the founder via WebSocket approval request.
- Review Founder Story, Mission, Vision, Market Thesis, Category Definition, and Strategic Positioning.
- *Rule: Alluci recommends. The founder decides.*
- **Output:** Approved Strategic Narrative.

### Step 10 — Publish & Integrate
- Commit approved memory objects into Alluci persistent memory (`polytope_data.db` / Graph Memory).
- Synchronize downstream workflows (Investor Pitch Deck, GTM, Recruiting, M&A).
- **Output:** Updated Organizational Intelligence & Synchronized Knowledge.

## 8. Failure Modes Diagnostic & 30-Question Completion Checklist

### Failure Mode Diagnostic Rules
Before finalizing strategic intelligence, Alluci checks for and self-corrects:
- **Chronology Trap:** Founder tells a resume instead of a strategic narrative.
- **Symptom Focus:** Company addresses symptoms instead of root causes.
- **Unvalidated Market Timing:** "Why Now" is poorly supported by evidence.
- **Category Confusion:** Category definition is vague or overlaps completely with incumbents.
- **Knowledge Silos:** Strategic knowledge remains trapped in conversations instead of memory graphs.
