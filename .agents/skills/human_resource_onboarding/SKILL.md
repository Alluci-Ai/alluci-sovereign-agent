# Human Resource Onboarding & Employee Integration (`hro_01`)

## 1. Overview & Operational Mandate

This skill defines the operational, cognitive, and procedural sequence for the **Alluci Sovereign Agent** to execute **Human Resource Onboarding & Employee Integration**.

### Strategic Purpose
An employee's first 90 days dictate long-term performance, cultural alignment, retention, and organizational productivity (`spe_01`, `swd_01`). Unstructured onboarding, missing legal documents (PIIA, Form I-9), delayed IT account provisioning, or ambiguous 30-60-90 day goals cause early turnover, compliance exposure (`ldl_01`), and inflated time-to-productivity (TTP).

**The Objective:** Transform static hiring handoffs into a frictionless, audit-ready **Employee Integration System** that automates pre-boarding compliance, IT account provisioning, peer buddy matching, 30-60-90 day milestone tracking, and Time-To-Productivity optimization.

$$\text{Pre-Boarding Audit} \longrightarrow \text{Day 1 Provisioning} \longrightarrow \text{30-60-90 Roadmap} \longrightarrow \text{TTP Calculation} \longrightarrow \text{Executive Approval} \longrightarrow \text{Vault Archive}$$

## 2. Core Values & Non-Negotiable Principles

When executing this skill, Alluci MUST strictly enforce the following 8 principles:

1. **Compliance Before Access:** Verify 100% execution of mandatory legal agreements (Offer Letter, PIIA, Form W-4, Form I-9) prior to granting full system access.
2. **Accelerated Time-To-Productivity (TTP):** Minimize days required for new hires to produce their first independent work output.
3. **Frictionless Provisioning:** Automate IT account creation, email/Slack access, and software tool licensing on or before Day 1.
4. **Structured 30-60-90 Day Milestones:** Define clear, measurable objectives for Day 30 (Learning), Day 60 (Role Competency), and Day 90 (Autonomous Output).
5. **Peer Mentorship & Belonging:** Pair every new hire with a dedicated onboarding buddy for cultural and technical guidance.
6. **Continuous Feedback Loops:** Conduct weekly 1:1 check-ins and 30/60/90-day formal performance reviews.
7. **Institutional Knowledge Integration:** Enroll new hires in relevant organizational memory modules (`okd_01` & `fde_01`).
8. **Audit-Ready Documentation:** Maintain complete, timestamped onboarding ledgers for legal, HR, and compliance audits (`ldl_01`).

## 4. Mathematical Models & Time-To-Productivity (TTP)

### 7-Tier Onboarding Decision Hierarchy
When orchestrating onboarding workflows or resolving friction, Alluci MUST prioritize:

$$\text{1. Legal \& Tax Compliance (PIIA / I-9)} \succ \text{2. Security \& Access Control} \succ \text{3. Time-To-Productivity (TTP)} \succ \text{4. Employee Experience} \succ \text{5. Milestone Clarity} \succ \text{6. Manager Bandwidth Efficiency} \succ \text{7. Process Automation}$$

### Time-To-Productivity (TTP) Formulation
For each new employee $e$:

$$TTP_e = \text{Date of First Independent Core Deliverable} - \text{Day 1 Start Date (Days)}$$

### Onboarding Efficiency Index ($OEI$)
Alluci evaluates onboarding program health across 4 weighted factors (0-100%):

$$OEI = (W_{\text{compliance}} \times S_{\text{compliance}}) + (W_{\text{milestones}} \times S_{\text{milestones}}) + (W_{\text{TTP}} \times S_{\text{TTP}}) + (W_{\text{satisfaction}} \times S_{\text{satisfaction}})$$

Where:
- $W_{\text{compliance}} = 0.35$ (100% PIIA, W-4, I-9, Offer Letter execution)
- $W_{\text{milestones}} = 0.25$ (On-time 30-60-90 day milestone achievement %)
- $W_{\text{TTP}} = 0.25$ (Actual TTP vs Target TTP ratio)
- $W_{\text{satisfaction}} = 0.15$ (New hire 30-day NPS survey score)

## 5. The 7-Step Operational Lifecycle Protocol

Alluci follows this operational sequence:

### Step 1 — Pre-Boarding Compliance & Provisioning Audit
- Verify executed offer letter, PIIA, Form W-4, and Form I-9 (`ldl_01`).
- Trigger automated IT account creation (Email, Slack, GitHub, HRIS, Jira).
- **Output:** Pre-Boarding Compliance & Provisioning Audit Report.

### Step 2 — Day 1 Orientation & Buddy Assignment
- Deliver welcome package, hardware credentials, and introduce assigned peer onboarding buddy.
- Review role responsibilities, team structure (`swd_01`), and company values.
- **Output:** Day 1 Orientation Confirmation.

### Step 3 — Synthesize 30-60-90 Day Milestone Roadmap
- Generate role-specific 30-day learning goals, 60-day competency targets, and 90-day autonomous output milestones (`spe_01`).
- Assign initial learning modules from Organizational Memory (`okd_01` & `fde_01`).
- **Output:** 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Roadmap.

### Step 4 — Milestone Tracking & 1:1 Check-in Monitoring
- Track weekly task completion, 30-day review, and 60-day milestone progress.
- Flag overdue deliverables or training friction in PCL daemon.
- **Output:** Milestone Progress Register.

### Step 5 — Time-To-Productivity (TTP) & OEI Evaluation
- Calculate TTP (Days to first independent output) and Onboarding Efficiency Index ($OEI$).
- Measure onboarding cost per hire (HR time + buddy time + IT tooling).
- **Output:** TTP & Onboarding Efficiency Report.

### Step 6 — Request Onboarding Completion Approval
- Broadcast WebSocket request to HR & hiring manager via `ExecApprovalManager` prior to finalizing 90-day probation period.
- *Rule: Alluci provisions, tracks, and scores. Human manager authorizes.*
- **Output:** Approved Onboarding Completion Log.

### Step 7 — Export Onboarding Management Package
- Export `Onboarding_Roadmap_Blueprint.json`, `Employee_Checklist_Ledger.csv`, `Day_30_60_90_Dashboard.html`, `Compliance_Verification_Report.md`, and `Onboarding_Manifest.json`.
- **Output:** Exported Onboarding Package & Vault Archive.

## 6. Automation Boundaries & Human-in-the-Loop Protocol

### Tier 1: AI-Assisted Activities (Autonomous Audit & Provisioning)
Alluci automatically handles:
- Legal document execution auditing (PIIA, Form W-4, Form I-9).
- Automated IT account provisioning requests & software licensing assignment.
- 30-60-90 day roadmap synthesis & learning module assignment (`okd_01`).
- Time-to-productivity ($TTP$) & Onboarding Efficiency Index ($OEI$) math.

### Tier 2: Human Approval Required (Mandatory Manager Sign-Off)
Alluci MUST pause and request explicit manager sign-off for:
- Authorizing 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day milestone completions.
- Passing or extending an employee's 90-day probationary period.
- Approving custom hardware or software subscription requests outside standard bands.
- Authorizing onboarding plan modifications or role scope changes.

### Tier 3: Autonomous Activities After Approval (System Synchronization)
Once manager sign-off is granted, Alluci automatically:
- Updates employee status to "Fully Integrated" in HRIS and vault memory.
- Refreshes workforce capacity metrics in `swd_01`.
- Registers 90-day performance review triggers in PCL daemon.
- Archives completed onboarding packages to vault deliverables.

## 7. Diagnostic & 30-Question Verification Checklist

### Failure Mode Diagnostic Rules
Alluci checks for and self-corrects:
- **Missing PIIA / Compliance Gap:** Allowing employee system access without signed PIIA agreement.
- **IT Provisioning Delays:** New hires waiting $> 24$ hours for email, Slack, or software access.
- **Ambiguous 30-60-90 Goals:** Onboarding without clear, measurable performance milestones.
- **Inflated TTP:** Employee taking $> 45$ days to complete initial core role deliverable.

### The 30-Question Completion Checklist
The workflow is complete ONLY when Alluci can answer all 30 questions:

#### Pre-Boarding & Compliance
- [ ] 100% of legal documents (Offer Letter, PIIA, Form W-4, Form I-9) executed (`ldl_01`)?
- [ ] Laptop hardware delivered and configured prior to Day 1?
- [ ] IT accounts (Email, Slack, GitHub, HRIS) fully provisioned?
- [ ] Dedicated peer onboarding buddy assigned and briefed?

#### 30-60-90 Day Roadmap & TTP
- [ ] 30-day learning & orientation goals defined?
- [ ] 60-day role competency targets established?
- [ ] 90-day autonomous output deliverables finalized (`spe_01`)?
- [ ] Time-To-Productivity ($TTP$) target established and monitored?

#### Governance & Analytics
- [ ] Onboarding Efficiency Index ($OEI$) calculated (0-100%)?
- [ ] Weekly 1:1 check-ins and 30/60/90-day reviews scheduled?
- [ ] Hiring manager approval obtained via WebSocket for 90-day completion?
- [ ] Audit-ready Onboarding Management package exported to vault?
