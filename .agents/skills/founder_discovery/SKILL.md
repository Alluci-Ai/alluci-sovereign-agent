# Founder Discovery & Narrative Development (`fnd_01`)

## 1. Overview & Operational Mandate

This skill defines the operational and cognitive sequence for the **Alluci Sovereign Agent** to execute **Founder Discovery & Narrative Development**. 

### Strategic Purpose
Most founders possess valuable experiences, hard-earned lessons, unique market observations, and deep convictions, but these insights often exist as disconnected fragments spread across conversations, customer interactions, product decisions, and years of professional experience:
- **Personal Foundation:** Childhood influences, personal values, defining experiences, career journey, major successes/failures, lessons learned.
- **Founder Journey:** Industry expertise, customer interactions, market observations, product decisions, turning points, conviction moments.
- **Company Evolution:** Why started, un-ignorable problems, customer revelations, solution evolution, strategic pivot decisions.
- **Future Vision:** Long-term aspirations, beliefs about the future, category evolution, customer transformation.

**The Objective:** Transform fragmented founder knowledge into a repeatable **Strategic Operating Narrative** that becomes the organization's single source of truth for pitch decks, investor meetings, customer messaging, recruiting, M&A positioning, product strategy, and long-term brand development.

## 2. Core Values & Non-Negotiable Principles

When executing this skill, Alluci MUST strictly adhere to the following 15 principles:

1. **Truth Over Hype:** Never sacrifice truth for persuasion.
2. **Authenticity Over Performance:** Genuine founder beliefs take precedence over generic startup copy.
3. **Accuracy Over Speed:** Validate claims rigorously before generating public assets.
4. **Evidence Over Unsupported Claims:** Require empirical or logical evidence for strategic assertions.
5. **Integrity Over Persuasion:** Maintain intellectual honesty in all market and competitor comparisons.
6. **Clarity Over Complexity:** Simplicity builds trust; complexity reduces clarity.
7. **Curiosity Over Assumptions:** Continually challenge conventional wisdom and default industry premises.
8. **Human-Centered Outcomes:** Focus on actual customer transformation and value creation.
9. **Customer Empathy:** Keep customer pain points and outcomes at the center of every narrative module.
10. **Founder Authenticity:** Highlight the founder's authentic voice, history, and convictions.
11. **Intellectual Honesty:** Explicitly acknowledge market risks, adoption barriers, and current gaps.
12. **Long-Term Trust Over Short-Term Attention:** Prioritize enduring credibility over clickbait headlines.
13. **Original Thinking Over Generic Positioning:** Avoid generic buzzwords; articulate original insights.
14. **Insight Over Buzzwords:** Define concrete mechanisms rather than abstract industry jargon.
15. **Strategic Honesty Over Marketing Language:** Use clear strategic rationale rather than exaggerated promotional speak.

## 4. Decision Framework & Evidence Matrix

### 7-Tier Decision Hierarchy
When evaluating competing strategic directions, narratives, or positioning options, Alluci MUST resolve tradeoffs using the following strict hierarchy:

$$\text{1. Truth} \succ \text{2. Founder Authenticity} \succ \text{3. Customer Value} \succ \text{4. Evidence} \succ \text{5. Strategic Differentiation} \succ \text{6. Long-Term Value} \succ \text{7. Simplicity}$$

### 5-Level Evidence & Validation Matrix
Every strategic recommendation and narrative claim generated by Alluci MUST be categorized and validated against the 5 evidence levels:

- **Level 1 — Founder Perspective:** Founder beliefs, personal experiences, vision, intuition. *(Useful for discovery; requires elevation).*
- **Level 2 — Customer Evidence:** Customer interviews, user feedback, surveys, discovery sessions, pilot metrics. *(Validates customer need).*
- **Level 3 — Market Evidence:** Industry reports, market research, competitive analysis, trend analytics. *(Validates market opportunity).*
- **Level 4 — Business Evidence:** Revenue, growth rate, retention, commercial partnerships, pipeline, expansion. *(Validates business viability).*
- **Level 5 — Strategic Evidence:** Repeatable customer outcomes, proven product-market fit, independent third-party validation, defensible competitive moat. *(Validates long-term enterprise value).*

## 5. The 10-Step Execution Protocol

Alluci follows this operational sequence from initiation to long-term memory capture:

### Step 1 — Initiate the Workflow
- Confirm workflow objectives and participating founder(s).
- Gather existing company materials (decks, whitepapers, interview transcripts).
- Define intended target audiences and desired output deliverables.
- **Output:** Structured Discovery Plan.

### Step 2 — Conduct Founder Discovery
- Execute structured discovery prompt sequences to capture personal background, career history, turning points, personal mission, and core convictions.
- Map childhood influences and defining failures/successes to current leadership philosophy.
- **Output:** Comprehensive Founder Profile.

### Step 3 — Discover Strategic Insights
- Analyze interview findings to extract contrarian market observations.
- Identify broken industry assumptions and recurring customer pain points.
- Core Question: *What does this founder understand that others have not yet recognized?*
- **Output:** Insight Inventory.

### Step 4 — Assess Founder-Market Fit
- Evaluate expertise, experience, network, credibility, timing, and unique access.
- Validate why this specific founder is uniquely qualified to solve this problem now.
- **Output:** Founder-Market Fit Assessment.

### Step 5 — Define Strategic Opportunity
- Articulate customer problem, market timing ("Why Now?"), technology/regulatory shifts, and category creation potential.
- Formulate the core Market Thesis.
- **Output:** Opportunity Architecture.

### Step 6 — Develop Narrative Engine
- Synthesize the **16 Modular Narratives** (see Section 6 schema).
- Ensure narrative flow connects personal origin to company mission and enterprise value.
- **Output:** Complete Narrative Library.

### Step 7 — Adapt for Each Audience
- Translate core narratives into audience-specific messaging for:
  - Investors (ROI, market size, defensibility, exit path)
  - Customers (Pain resolution, outcomes, ease of adoption)
  - Employees/Talent (Mission, growth, culture, long-term impact)
  - Partners/Acquirers (Synergy, strategic value creation)
- **Output:** Audience-Specific Messaging Repository.

### Step 8 — Audit & Validate
- Run the **5-Level Evidence Audit** across all claims.
- Verify authenticity with founder and confirm strategic differentiation against competitors.
- Audit against the **23 Required Questions Checklist**.
- **Output:** Approved Strategic Narrative.

### Step 9 — Generate Deliverables
- Produce executive summaries, founder biographies, pitch deck content, website copy, data room narratives, and recruiting materials.
- **Output:** Strategic Communication Assets.

### Step 10 — Capture Organizational Knowledge
- Store founder insights, approved narratives, and domain terms into Alluci persistent memory (`polytope_data.db` / `H-LSM`).
- Link outputs to downstream execution workflows (Investor Pitch Deck Skill, GTM Strategy, PR).
- **Output:** Updated Organizational Knowledge Base.

## 6. The 16-Part Modular Narrative Schema

Alluci generates and maintains 16 distinct modular narrative blocks:

1. **Founder Story:** The authentic personal trajectory and personal mission of the founder.
2. **Origin Story:** The spark, revelation, or defining moment that forced the company into existence.
3. **Insight Narrative:** The non-obvious truth or contrarian realization about the market.
4. **Market Shift Narrative:** The macroeconomic, technological, or behavioral tectonic shift occurring now.
5. **Why Now Narrative:** The convergence of forces making this solution viable today when it was impossible 5 years ago.
6. **Problem Narrative:** The acute, costly, or unsolved pain point experienced by customers.
7. **Solution Narrative:** The fundamental breakthrough that solves the root cause rather than symptoms.
8. **Product Narrative:** How the solution manifests as concrete, usable software/hardware features.
9. **Category Narrative:** The newly defined or redefined market category being established.
10. **Market Narrative:** The total addressable opportunity and market expansion trajectory.
11. **Business Model Narrative:** How value is captured, monetized, and scaled sustainably.
12. **Traction Narrative:** Empirical proof of momentum, customer love, and product adoption.
13. **Competitive Narrative:** Why current incumbents and alternative workarounds fail.
14. **Strategic Value Narrative:** The compounding moat, data flywheels, or workflow lock-in created.
15. **Acquisition Narrative:** Why the company represents an indispensable strategic asset for potential acquirers.
16. **Vision Narrative:** The 10-year future state created when this company achieves its mission.

## 8. Failure Modes Diagnostic & 23-Question Completion Checklist

### Failure Mode Diagnostic Rules
Before finalizing any narrative asset, Alluci scans for and fixes:
- **Chronology Trap:** Founder tells a chronological resume instead of a strategic narrative. *(Fix: Structure around problem-revelation-insight).*
- **Generic Positioning:** Claims could apply equally to a competitor. *(Fix: Push for unique mechanism and Level 3+ evidence).*
- **Feature Focus:** Focuses on product specs instead of customer outcomes. *(Fix: Re-frame using Jobs-to-be-Done).*
- **Unvalidated Claims:** Level 1 founder beliefs presented as market facts. *(Fix: Flag as assumption or attach Level 2/3 evidence).*
- **Vague "Why Now":** Fails to explain timing catalysts. *(Fix: Identify specific tech/regulatory/economic triggers).*

The workflow is complete ONLY when Alluci can answer all 23 questions with clean evidence:

- [ ] Why this founder?

- [ ] Why is this founder uniquely positioned?

#### Problem

#### Opportunity

- [ ] What assumptions are breaking?

#### Company

#### Market & Strategic Value
